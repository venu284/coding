## Drill Types

1. **Template drill:** type the template from memory, then compare with `03_template_bank.py`.
2. **Recognition drill:** read a problem title and constraints, name the concept in under 2 minutes.
3. **Anchor solve:** solve the anchor from scratch with tests.
4. **Variation solve:** solve one extra problem without topic labels.
5. **Explain-back:** explain why the concept works in 60 seconds.

## Spaced Repetition

After first solving an anchor, re-solve it from scratch on:

- Day 1
- Day 3
- Day 7
- Day 21

If you cannot identify the concept or template quickly, reset that anchor to Day 1.
